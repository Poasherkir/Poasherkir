## 👋 Hey, I'm Malik!

<img align="right" width="230" src="https://github.com/Poasherkir.png" alt="Malik's avatar" />

I'm a Computer Science student from **Algeria** 🇩🇿.
I build apps that work anywhere, even on a cheap phone with no signal.

- 📱 Mobile apps with **Flutter**
- 🌐 Websites with **Next.js** and **TypeScript** (plus some 3D when I get the chance)
- 🐍 **Python** scripts for the boring jobs
- 🔒 Private work: **Briefing Point Go**, **TechSub** and a few aviation apps. [More on my site](https://malikboudine.vercel.app)
- 🤝 Open to **internships** and **freelance** work
- 📫 **malikboudinee1e@gmail.com**

<br clear="right" />

### 🧠 How I build stuff

- 📴 **Offline first.** My apps keep working without internet, and your data stays encrypted on the phone.
- 🌍 **Arabic and French from day one.** Right-to-left layouts included.
- ✅ **Tests must pass.** If it only works on my laptop, it's not done.
- 🐢 **Made for slow phones.** Small, fast and easy on the battery.

### 🎲 Random facts

- I wrote a Wordle solver in C. Yes, that's cheating.
- I also wrote a script that downloads whole YouTube playlists.
- An arrow pointing the wrong way in an Arabic app bugs me way more than it should.
